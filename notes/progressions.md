# Progression lens

Free selection compares chords as a set. A progression is different: it is an
ordered sequence and may revisit the same chord. Harmonia therefore keeps the
two models separate.

The first progression library is deliberately small and diatonic to a major
key. Its members are expressed as Roman numerals, so a preset transposes with
the selected major key:

- I–V–vi–IV
- I–vi–IV–V
- ii–V–I
- I–IV–V–I
- vi–IV–I–V
- I–vi–ii–V

The circle marks every ordered chord view with its ordinal number. Progression
presets additionally draw directed links between successive roots. The keyboard
and chord list preserve that exact sequence, including repeated arrivals.

This lens intentionally makes no static claim about functional harmony. Future
work may compare progressions by exact properties such as rotation, shared
chord sets, and structural substitutions before adding a convention-specific
functional reading.
