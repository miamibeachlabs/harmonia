# Future lens: harmonic function in progressions

Harmonia deliberately does not assign every chord a permanent functional hue.
Tonic, predominant, and dominant roles depend on harmonic language and on what
the chord does in a progression, especially in natural minor.

When this lens returns, it should:

- derive function from an explicit selected progression rather than a static node;
- distinguish natural-minor modal motion from raised-leading-tone minor cadence;
- keep root identity hue separate from functional behavior; and
- name the musical style or convention whose function rules are in use.
